# POL-20 Data Classification and Handling Policy
(Synthetic internal policy for testing; not legal advice.)

## §1 Classes
Public, Internal, Confidential (for example pricing, sales pipeline, contracts), Restricted (customer personal data, payment data, credentials).

## §2 Confidential data
Confidential data must not be stored in personal email or personal cloud storage. Bulk exports of confidential data require a business reason and are limited to roles that need them.

## §3 Restricted data
Restricted data must be encrypted at rest, access-logged and never publicly accessible.

## §4 Incidents involving restricted data
If restricted data may have been accessed by an unauthorised party, the privacy and legal team must be informed within 4 hours of the security team becoming aware, so they can assess notification duties. Severity is at least SEV1 when exposure of restricted data at scale is confirmed.

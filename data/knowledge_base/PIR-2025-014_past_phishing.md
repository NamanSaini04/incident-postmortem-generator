# PIR-2025-014 Past Incident Review: Mailbox Takeover via Phishing
(Synthetic historical record for testing.)

## §1 Summary
In 2025 a finance user entered credentials on a look-alike login page. The attacker signed in from abroad and set up an inbox rule hiding replies from a supplier.

## §2 Lessons
The impossible-travel alert was medium severity and waited behind higher alerts for 40 minutes. Agreed action: raise impossible-travel alerts on accounts without MFA to high and triage alerts for the same user together.

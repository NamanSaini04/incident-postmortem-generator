# PB-05 Data Exposure and Insider Playbook
(Synthetic internal playbook for testing.)

## §1 Stop the exposure
Remove public access or suspend the user's access first.

## §2 Who accessed the data
Use access logs to list every party that accessed the data during the exposure window; do not assume the reporter was the only one.

## §3 Insider cases
Involve HR and legal before interviewing the employee, and preserve logs and devices first.

## §4 Deletion and copies
Obtain a written deletion attestation and verify it where possible; record any copies that cannot be confirmed deleted as a remaining risk.

## §5 Classification and notification
Classify the data under POL-20 and follow POL-20 §4 if restricted data is involved.

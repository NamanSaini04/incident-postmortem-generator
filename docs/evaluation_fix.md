## Measures that need care

- **Recommendation relevance is a proxy.** It checks an action is tied to evidence behind a finding. It cannot tell a good fix from a weak one; that needs a security reviewer.
- **Unsupported claim rate counts what the validator caught**, not what it missed. Claims that cite a real record but misread it pass the validator; the hand-checked hallucination traps cover part of that gap.
- **Older runs predate later stages** (ATT&CK in Phase 7; impact and response timing in Phase 8). Those measures are n/a for them and left out of the means and of completeness, instead of counting as 0%.
- **Consistency from a few runs** shows whether answers move, not how often.
